Overview

A small Unity 3D platform-survival game, built as a gameplay prototype for the Hitwicket game-developer challenge. The player walks across a chain of platforms ("pulpits") that appear, count down and disappear. Reaching new platforms earns points, which unlock a single jump and then a double jump. Falling ends the run. Normal mode has a target score and a victory screen; Endless mode keeps going. The game is playable in the browser as a WebGL build.

Implementation

  • Movement (PlayerMovement.cs). Rigidbody-based, camera-relative input, with the character rotated to face the direction of travel. Jumps are counted and gated by ScoreManager: no jump before the first threshold, then a single jump, then a double jump.
  • Camera (ThirdPersonCamera.cs). A spring-damper follow camera. Acceleration is stiffness × displacement − damping × velocity (stiffness 20, damping 2), integrated each frame, so the camera trails the player with momentum instead of snapping.
  • Platforms (Pulpit.cs, PulpitSpawner.cs). Each platform takes its lifetime from the JSON range and scales it as the score passes thresholds. It fades in and out with coroutines and shows a world-space countdown. Platform types are static, side-to-side, diagonal and up-down, and each type has its own material. The next platform spawns in a random direction other than back toward the previous one, before the current one disappears.
  • Game flow. GameManager handles modes, game over, victory and restart. ScoreManager handles scoring and unlock thresholds. AudioManager centralises music and effects.
  • Configuration. Player speed and platform timing load from Resources/doofus_diary.json through DoofusDiaryLoader, not from constants in code.
