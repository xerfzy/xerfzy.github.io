About

I like understanding things at the level where the hardware and the math meet. Most of my time goes into computer graphics and GPU computing — writing shaders, building small renderers, and figuring out how to make parallel hardware do exactly what I want it to.

I work mainly in C++, and I'm drawn to problems where correctness and performance both matter: physics simulation, rendering pipelines, and the systems-level code that holds them together.

Outside of that, I spend my time on calisthenics and bouldering, usually with a coffee nearby and a cat somewhere in the room.
